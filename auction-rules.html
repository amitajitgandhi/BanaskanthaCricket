Auction Rules

Know your purse. Plan your squad. Bid smart.

Official auction rules for all 16 teams.

Platinum+ players

  • Platinum+ players are special players within the Platinum category, highlighted as premium players.
  • Platinum+ is not a separate squad category.
  • Any team may bid for any Platinum or Platinum+ player.
  • Teams may acquire multiple Platinum+ players. There is no requirement for every team to select exactly one.
  • Retained players count toward the required category slots.

04Bidding Rules−
  • Bidding starts at the applicable category's base price.
  • Every bid increase must be in multiples of 1,000 points or more. Valid increases include 1,000, 2,000, 3,000, 5,000 points and so on.
  • Bidders may jump directly to a much higher bid, provided the increase is a multiple of 1,000 points and the team has sufficient available purse after required reservations.
  • Once the auctioneer declares a player sold, the winning bid is final.
  • Teams must comply with the mandatory purse-reservation rule before placing a bid.
05Booster Rules−
  • Each team may purchase a maximum of 5 boosters.
  • Each booster costs ₹1,000 in actual money and adds 10,000 points to the team's auction purse.
  • Teams may purchase boosters individually or all five together.
  • Boosters may be purchased at any time before the relevant player's name and photograph appear on screen.
  • Once the player's name and photograph are displayed, no booster can be purchased for that bidding round.
  • The maximum of five boosters applies separately to each team.
06Mandatory Purse Reservation−

Every team must reserve enough points to fill all its remaining mandatory player slots at their applicable base prices.

  • Before placing a bid, account for the proposed bid and reserve the base-price amount for every other unfilled slot.
  • The reserve is calculated only for unfilled slots other than the player currently being auctioned.
  • A category that is already complete requires no further reserve.
  • As players are purchased and categories are completed, the reserve must be recalculated.

07Last-Player Allocation & Unsold Players−

Players who remain unsold in the initial auction will be offered once again in one repeat auction round. Players still unsold after the repeat round will be allocated through a spin wheel, subject to the category rules below.

Platinum, including Platinum+

  • The last player in the Platinum lot will automatically be allotted to the only team with an unfilled Platinum slot.
  • The allocation price is the average final sale price of the three lowest-priced players sold in the Platinum category, rounded to the nearest 1,000 points.
  • A player unsold in the initial auction will be offered once again in the repeat auction round.
  • If still unsold after the repeat round, the player will be allocated through a spin wheel among eligible teams at the same average-based allocation price.

Gold

  • The last player in the Gold lot will automatically be allotted to the only team with an unfilled Gold slot.
  • The allocation price is the average final sale price of the three lowest-priced players sold in the Gold category, rounded to the nearest 1,000 points.
  • A player unsold in the initial auction will be offered once again in the repeat auction round.
  • If still unsold after the repeat round, the player will be allocated through a spin wheel among eligible teams at the same average-based allocation price.

Silver

  • The last player in the Silver lot will automatically be allotted to the only team with an unfilled Silver slot at the Silver base price of 3,000 points.
  • A player unsold in the initial auction will be offered once again in the repeat auction round.
  • If still unsold after the repeat round, the player will be allocated through a spin wheel among eligible teams at the Silver base price of 3,000 points.

Allocation conditions

  • Only teams with an unfilled slot in the relevant category are eligible for spin-wheel allocation.
  • Teams must have sufficient purse to pay the allocation price while maintaining the required reserve for their other unfilled slots.
  • No team may exceed its category composition or 10-player squad limit.
08 Committee Decisions & Conduct +
  • The CLBPL Committee's decision shall be final in all auction-related matters.
  • No arguments or disputes against the Committee's final decisions will be entertained.
  • All participants are requested to cooperate with the Committee and support its decisions in the best interest of the tournament.
  • If you have any questions or doubts, please get them clarified with the Committee.
