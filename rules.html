🏏 Official Tournament Rules

SBKYM Premier League
Season 4

Season 4 • 11–13 December 2026

League Stage

Each team plays 3 matches. Top 8 teams advance to the Super 8 knockout round.

Top Bracket — Teams ranked 1–4 compete for direct semifinal spots.
Bottom Bracket — Teams ranked 5–8 compete for remaining semifinal opportunities.

🔝 Top Bracket

TB1 — Rank 1 vs Rank 4 → SF1
TB2 — Rank 2 vs Rank 3 → SF2

⬇️ Bottom Bracket

BB1 — Rank 5 vs Rank 8
BB2 — Rank 6 vs Rank 7

🔀 Elimination

TB1 Loser vs BB2 Winner → SF3
TB2 Loser vs BB1 Winner → SF4

🥊 Semis & Final

SF1 vs SF4  |  SF2 vs SF3
→ GRAND FINAL 🏆

⚡ Powerplay Over — Over 3 (2.1 to 2.6)
  • Fixed at the 3rd over of each innings.
⭐ Magic Ball Rule — Over 8 (7.1 to 7.6)
  • All 6 balls of the 8th over are Magic Balls.
  • All runs (extras, through bat, byes, leg byes, overthrow) are doubled.
  • Wicket falls: –5 runs penalty.
  • Wicket on wide / no-ball: no runs counted (including extras) + flat –5 penalty.
  • Strike rotation follows actual runs scored, not doubled runs.
Boundary Rules
  • Front net boundary only — side nets do not count as boundary.
  • Side net catches → NOT OUT
  • Ceiling net catches → OUT
Ball Out of Play / Dead Ball
  • Ball outside turf or any unreachable area → 1 declared run
  • Ball resting on ceiling net then falls → Dead Ball
  • Batsmen must change strike for next delivery (bat only — not byes or overthrow).
Opening Batter Rule League Only
  • In 3 league matches: 3, 3, and 4 new batsmen start each game.
  • If a player is repeated in the top 3 and notified by opponent during the innings:
    • That player is declared OUT
    • All runs scored by them are DEDUCTED
  • This rule does not apply in knockout matches.
Byes & Leg Byes

All byes & leg byes are allowed, even when no shot is offered.

Runner Rules

Only one runner is allowed per innings.

Retired Hurt

Retired Hurt is treated as retired out and cannot return to bat again.

Hit Wicket
  • Dislodging stumps with bat/body/clothing before ball is dead = OUT
  • Hit a boundary/six but also hit wicket → hit wicket takes precedence → OUT
  • On Magic Ball: team also loses 5 runs
Normal Overs
  • Area behind bowling crease divided into two equal halves by a partition line.
  • Each half can have max 2 fielders.
  • All others must remain ahead of the bowling crease.
⚡ Powerplay Fielding
  • Max 1 fielder on the boundary.
  • Max 2 fielders between bowling crease and partition line.
  • All others must remain ahead of the bowling crease.

⚠️ Fielding team talking while ball is bowled — 1st: Warning. Next: No-Ball.

Every Player Bowls 1 Over — No Repeat Overs

Each player bowls exactly 1 over. No bowler may repeat unless forced by team shortage rules.

Speed Ball & Hand Rotation Rule
  • First Instance:
    • Results in dot ball or wicket → Dead Ball + Warning
    • Runs are scored → Warning only
  • Any further instance in the same innings → No-Ball

📢 Only the striker may appeal within 5 seconds. Non-strikers and players outside the pitch are not allowed to appeal. Final decision rests solely with the umpire.

Dead Ball Rule

A ball that bounces twice before reaching the striker is Dead Ball. Exception: if the second bounce occurs behind the batter, the delivery is legal.

Bowling Box Rule
  • Touching side or back border → No-Ball
  • Fully crossing the front line / overstepping → No-Ball
Side & Keeper Confirmation
  • Bowler must confirm bowling side with umpire before every over or before switching.
  • Change in wicket-keeper must be confirmed with the umpire.
  • Failure to confirm → No-Ball
Stumping Rule

All kinds of stumping are allowed except when the ball is collected ahead of the stump — that will result in a No-Ball.

No-Ball Consequences

Every no-ball is followed by a Free Hit. On a Free Hit, only Run Out will be considered.

Scoring Requirement

One batting member must sit with the scorer during their innings. Disputes must be raised immediately.

Tiebreaker Rules

League:

  1. Most Sixes
  2. Most Fours
  3. Fewer Wickets Lost

Knockout:

Super Over

Team Short of Players
  • Minimum 6 players required to start. Fewer than 6 → opponent receives a Bye.
  • Batting first: Any late-arriving player may join if they arrive before the end of the first innings.
  • Bowling first: All players must be present before the first ball. Otherwise:
    • A committee-provided substitute fielder — may field anywhere in front of the bowling crease, not as wicketkeeper.
    • Only one committee substitute provided, regardless of how many players are missing.
    • A dead runner for the last wicket.
Short Player Bowling Allocation
  • 1 short: Any 1 of the two bowlers who conceded the most runs will bowl the 10th over.
  • 2+ short: After all available players bowl their mandatory 1 over, the two bowlers who conceded the most runs will bowl remaining overs alternately.

📌 For this calculation, the Magic Over bowler's runs are counted as actual runs, not doubled.

  • Team jersey is mandatory.
  • Fiber bats are NOT allowed
  • Only sponsored caps permitted on the field.
Umpire's Authority

The Umpire's decision is final and binding in all cases.

Any argument with the umpire will result in Warning followed by Match Ban.

Committee Rights

The Tournament Committee reserves the right to revise, amend, or interpret any rule as necessary for the fair conduct of the tournament.

🏏

All standard ICC rules will apply wherever not specifically mentioned in these tournament rules.

All players and teams are expected to always respect the spirit of the game and the authority of the officials.
